Sharped

Sharp-edged gradients for content and webpages.

SHAPE·COLOUR·EXPORT

Choose a colour or make your own.

Shape and cross the lines

Select Line 1 or Line 2, then drag anywhere on the canvas to move it. Start near the top or bottom end of the selected line to change its angle. Lines can cross for sharper, layered compositions.

Use it anywhere

Pick a preset or custom colour, then download the background as PNG, JPG, WebP, or scalable SVG. Choose a ready-made size for social posts, Substack, Medium, stories, or desktop.
